# Munroe Island Tourism & Boating Context

A localized backwater tourism discovery and booking platform connecting visitors with native boatmen and operators in Munroe Island (Munroethuruthu), Kerala.

## Language

### Core Entities

**Customer**:
The tourist or travel party booking or inquiring about boating services.  
_Avoid_: Client, User, Passenger, Guest.

**Boatman**:
The local licensed operator or punter who maneuvers the boat and guides the customer through the waterways.  
_Avoid_: Driver, Pilot, Captain, Vendor.

**Experience**:
A specific bookable boating journey (e.g., Sunrise Canoe Tour, Sunset Shikara Cruise) with defined duration, route, and capacity.  
_Avoid_: Package, Product, Service, Activity.

**Vessel**:
The physical watercraft categorized by canal capability (Canoe, Shikara, Kayak).  
_Avoid_: Boat, Vehicle, Transport.

### Booking & Financials

**Booking**:
A confirmed or pending customer reservation containing guest count, date, selected time preference, and calculated pricing.  
_Avoid_: Order, Ticket, Trip, Reservation.

**Token Advance**:
The mandatory 25% online upfront fee paid by the customer to lock in their booking slot.  
_Avoid_: Deposit, Partial payment, Down payment.

**Jetty Balance**:
The remaining 75% payment amount settled in person by the customer directly with the boatman upon arrival at the jetty.  
_Avoid_: Due amount, Final bill, Cash on delivery.

**Lead**:
An unconfirmed direct WhatsApp inquiry initiated by a customer with pre-filled trip parameters.  
_Avoid_: Prospect, Contact, Query.

### Logistics & Waterways

**Jetty**:
The specific waterfront boarding and disembarkation point assigned for a booked experience.  
_Avoid_: Dock, Pier, Port, Harbor.

**Time Window**:
The preferred part of the day selected by the customer (Sunrise, Morning, Afternoon, Sunset) prior to exact slot confirmation.  
_Avoid_: Slot, Schedule, Timetable.

**Canal Access**:
The physical capability of a vessel to navigate shallow, narrow mangrove-shaded waterways and pass under low bridges.  
_Avoid_: Narrow route, Small river access.
